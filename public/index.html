V2.2 first captures the composition locally, then rebuilds the final video frame-by-frame with exact WebCodecs timestamps. At 60 FPS, a 6 second export contains exactly 360 timed frames. When Chrome asks what to share, choose This Tab / the current HTML to Video tab, then press Share.

Requested output: WEBM · 1920×1080 · 6 seconds. V2.2 also suppresses the tiny capture-handshake black prefix when detected. The deterministic encoder currently targets WebM VP9/VP8; MP4 requests may fall back to WebM or to the V2.1 compatibility encoder depending on browser support.
